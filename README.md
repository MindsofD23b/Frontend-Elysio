## Management Summary

Elysio is a web-based dating application concept developed within a simulated startup environment. The objective is to design and plan a **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)** that addresses common issues in modern dating platforms such as fake profiles, low trust, and endless text-based communication.

Instead of relying on swiping and prolonged messaging, Elysio connects users directly through **real-time video interaction**, enabling immediate and authentic first impressions.

The project simulates startup conditions, focusing on product planning, prioritization, teamwork, and documentation to produce a realistic and professionally structured MVP concept.

## Project Background

### Why this project exists

Many dating platforms feel repetitive and artificial. Users invest time in conversations that often do not lead to real connections. This results in frustration and declining trust in digital dating solutions.

The project explores whether digital dating can become more authentic and human by prioritizing real-time interaction.

### School and Startup Context

Developed as part of the IPT-9.1 module, the project follows a startup-style workflow where the team independently organizes planning, responsibilities, documentation, and outcomes.

---

## The Idea Behind Elysio

### Problem with Current Dating Apps

Most platforms rely on profile images and text chats, often leading to unrealistic expectations and fake profiles. Conversations frequently end without meeting in real life.

### Our Solution

Elysio introduces instant video-based matching, allowing users to see and speak with each other immediately, encouraging natural communication.

### What Makes Elysio Different

Elysio removes unnecessary complexity and focuses on authenticity:

- No endless swiping
- No heavily filtered profiles
- Immediate real interaction
- Fast and respectful matching flow

## Project Goals

### Main Goal

Design and document a functional MVP that clearly demonstrates the concept of Elysio.

### SMART Goals

- Deliver a working MVP concept within the project timeline
- Provide structured documentation and prototype designs
- Demonstrate feasibility through clear product definition
- Focus strictly on essential features

---

## Product Scope

### Core MVP Features

- User onboarding and account creation
- Basic profile setup
- Matching flow
- Live video interaction
- Clean and intuitive user interface

### Features Intentionally Excluded

To maintain focus, advanced features such as complex monetization systems, large moderation infrastructures, and extensive backend systems are excluded from the MVP scope.
